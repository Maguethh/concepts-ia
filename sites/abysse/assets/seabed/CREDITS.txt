Abysse - seabed assets (site/public/assets/seabed/)

Poly Haven textures, CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://polyhaven.com - resized to 1024 px and re-encoded as WebP:
  rockA_*.webp  <- seaside_rock          https://polyhaven.com/a/seaside_rock
  rockB_*.webp  <- rock_surface          https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_surface
  rockC_*.webp  <- worn_rock_natural_01  https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_rock_natural_01  (lichen spots removed, desaturated)
  rust_*.webp   <- rust_coarse_01        https://polyhaven.com/a/rust_coarse_01
  (albedo = diffuse, normal = nor_gl (OpenGL), rough = rough)

Generated for this project (no outside material):
  sand_albedo.webp, sand_normal.webp, sand_rough.webp - procedural, tileable (abysse/seabed/prep_tex.py)
  seabed.glb - terrain, rocks, wreck, flora and brittle stars modelled procedurally in Blender (abysse/seabed/seabed.py)
